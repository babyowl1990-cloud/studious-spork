Floating Glass Panel

Put on your headset, open this page in the Meta Quest Browser, then tap the button below. A glass panel with a live clock will appear floating in your real room.

This browser/device doesn't report AR support. This only works in a WebXR-AR capable browser (e.g. the Meta Quest Browser on a Quest headset) — and the page needs to be served over https (or localhost), not opened as a local file.
